What the data showed
  • 88% of spend (EGP 2,591) went to one keyword with 161 clicks, so the factory's coated sheet product barely appeared.
  • A "for sale" keyword variant cost EGP 5.60 per click, under a third of the others, and shows clear buying intent.
  • Three sitelinks led to site-search pages and one was disapproved.
Next steps
  • Test a higher bid on the "for sale" keyword.
  • Replace the search-page sitelinks with real product pages and review the rejection.
  • Add phone call and conversion tracking. Call button clicks are not calls yet, so I report them as clicks.

Account audit and plan · Decorative lighting e-commerce · Egypt

Audit first, then a four-ad-set sales plan

What the audit found
  • Women audiences produced zero purchases over two months, after more than EGP 2,000 of spend.
  • A campaign named Sales optimized for conversations, which sent EGP 5,080 to the wrong goal.
  • Cost per 1,000 impressions rose 3.5x while frequency stayed near 2.1 to 2.4, which points to creative fatigue.
The plan
  • EGP 20,000 for one month across four ad sets: interests 40%, lookalike 25%, retargeting 20%, catalog retargeting 15%.
  • Creative refreshed every 5 to 7 days. Ad sets stop when click-through falls under 1%.
  • A test budget capped at 5 to 10% to retry women with new creative.
Plan · Industrial manufacturer (B2B) · Egypt

Quote requests from contractors and factories

  • Built around how industrial buyers decide: specify, compare quotes, then decide with several people involved.
  • EGP 20,000 over 30 days. Meta 60% (EGP 12,000, split 30% credibility and 70% lead messages), Google 40% (EGP 8,000).
  • Six content pillars led by education at 25%, and sales posts that ask for product type, thickness, quantity and project location.
  • Targets: click-through 1.5% to 3% and landing page conversion of 5% or more. Cost per lead is reviewed after launch.
Plan · Catering and event styling brand · Cairo

Two projects instead of one ad

  • Cheese platters (fast decision) and catering, styling and stations (large, slow decision) get separate ads, audiences and WhatsApp messages.
  • EGP 10,000 per month. A 10-day test with EGP 3,000, then budget moves to the campaign that produced bookings. Each ad runs in two video versions and is reviewed every 3 days.
  • Closing plan: an auto-reply asking for date, guests and venue, three clear packages, a time-limited offer and a 5-minute reply rule.
  • Targets of EGP 20 to 40 per message and 4 to 6% message-to-booking are estimates, not promises.
Plan · Seafood and Lebanese restaurant · Riyadh

How I work

The same four steps on every account.

  1. STEP 1

    Test

    One campaign with several creatives. In the first 7 days I see which ad brings messages from people who are actually interested.

  2. STEP 2

    Review every 3 days

    I pause weak ads and move the budget to the ads that bring better results.

  3. STEP 3

    Support the close

    Quick replies and clear packages and prices are what turn inquiries into bookings.

  4. STEP 4

    Report weekly

    Three numbers: what we spent, how many inquiries came in, and how many bookings were confirmed.

I don't promise a set number of bookings up front, because results depend on many factors. I work from the actual numbers and keep improving them.
